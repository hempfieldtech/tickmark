# Tasks and states

## Groceries #sync
A `#sync` heading sends every checklist item under it to your task list (when sync is on).

- [ ] Oat milk
- [ ] Coffee beans 📅 2030-01-15
- [x] Bread

## Checkbox states

- [ ] To do
- [/] In progress (right-click a box in the preview to pick a state)
- [x] Done
- [-] Cancelled

## Single tasks

- [ ] Renew the passport #task ⏫ 📅 2030-03-01
- [ ] Water the plants #task 🔁 every week

A `#task` tag on one item syncs just that item; ⏫ 🔼 🔽 set priority, 📅 a due date and 🔁 a repeat. Typing `:calendar:` or `:repeat:` inserts them.

## Sub-items

- [ ] Plan the trip
  - [x] Pick the dates
  - [ ] Book the train

A task with open sub-items can't be completed until they're done.
