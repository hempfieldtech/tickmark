Tickmark

Open a note from the sidebar, or press Ctrl+P.

Tag a checklist item with #task, or put #sync on a heading, to send it to your CalDAV task list.

Properties

The note's frontmatter. Nothing changes until you press Save. Lists are comma-separated.

Settings

CalDAV accounts

Each account syncs with one task list. A checklist item goes to the account that owns its tag: an item tag (- [ ] Buy milk #task) wins over a section tag on a heading (## Groceries #sync). Each tag belongs to one account.

🔒 Saved encrypted on the server, with your SSO client secret as the key.

Sync

Also understood on checklist items: 📅 2026-11-25, due:2026-11-25 14:00, ⏫ 🔼 🔽, 🔁 every week.

Theme for this device. It applies straight away.

Theme editor

Make your own theme by picking colours; no CSS needed. Your themes follow you to every device you sign in on and show up in the list above.

Custom CSS

Your own styles, on top of the theme, on every device you sign in on. Changes show as you type; Save keeps them. Handy variables: --accent --bg --surface --panel --text --muted --line --sans --mono --radius. If something goes wrong, open Tickmark with ?nocss on the end of the address to load it without them.

Branding

The name and pictures this Tickmark shows everyone: in the sidebar, on the sign-in screen, in browser tabs, and as the installed app. Only admins can change them.

Logo

Sidebar, sign-in screen and app icon. A square image, 512×512 or an SVG, works best.

Favicon

The browser tab icon. Leave it on the default to use the logo.
